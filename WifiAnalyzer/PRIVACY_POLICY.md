# Privacy Policy – WiFi Analyzer

_Last updated: October 2026_

WiFi Analyzer ("the app") helps you test your internet speed and analyze your Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what the app processes and why.

## Information processed on your device
- **Wi-Fi details:** network name, signal strength, IP addresses, router address, DNS and nearby networks, read through Android's Wi-Fi APIs.
- **Devices on your network:** the device scanner checks addresses on your local network to list connected devices. Names you give devices and your trusted list are stored only on your phone.
- **Speed test history and heatmaps:** stored only on your phone. You can delete them at any time.
- **Data usage:** if you grant usage access, the app reads Android's data usage statistics to show them to you. They never leave your device.

We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect, sell or share this information.

## Location permission
Android requires location permission for apps to read Wi-Fi names and scan results. The app uses it only for that purpose and never stores or transmits your location.

## Speed tests
Speed tests connect to Cloudflare's public speed test service (speed.cloudflare.com). Like any website, Cloudflare receives your public IP address. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

## Advertising
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and app usage to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy choices. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.
