# Privacy Policy — Universal Remote

_Last updated: October 2026_

Universal Remote ("the app") lets you control your smart TV from your Android phone over your local Wi-Fi network.

## Information the app uses

- **Local network information.** The app searches your Wi-Fi network for TVs and talks to them directly. TV names, IP addresses, MAC addresses and pairing keys are stored **only on your phone** and are never sent to us.
- **Advertising.** The free version shows interstitial and rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location from your IP address and ad-interaction data to show and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time in **Settings › Privacy choices**. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
- **Purchases.** Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, store or sell personal data on our own servers.
- We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone or location.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions? Contact us through the Google Play listing.
